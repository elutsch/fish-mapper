# Lake Primer

## Physical character
Reservoir on the Welland River behind the Binbrook dam, in Binbrook (City of Hamilton). Created in 1971 to augment summer river flow and provide seasonal flood control; the largest inland lake in the Niagara Peninsula watershed.

## Morphology signal
Mid-sized, shallow, warm reservoir — long and narrow with many small islands and weedy offshoots. Nutrient-rich, wind-exposed water that gets choppy in a blow; stained to turbid rather than clear. Classic shallow-and-weedy character, not a deep clear basin.

## Watershed & access
Part of the Welland River system in the Niagara Peninsula, managed by the Niagara Peninsula Conservation Authority as the Binbrook Conservation Area. Public boat launch at the conservation area with paddlecraft/electric-motor access and a day-use fee.

## Managing authorities
- Niagara Peninsula Conservation Authority (NPCA)
- Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources (FMZ 16)

## Fishery reputation signal
Well-known Hamilton-area fishing hot spot that hosts spring and ice-fishing derbies. Known for largemouth bass, crappie, and panfish in the weedy shallows, with smallmouth bass, northern pike, walleye, and yellow perch also present.
