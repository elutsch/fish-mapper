# Species Fit: Lake Niapenco

## Summary
- **Destination species:** (none)
- **Strong species:** Largemouth Bass, Black Crappie
- **Headline structure surfaced:** weedy shallow bays and the many small islands and offshoots of a shallow, warm, nutrient-rich reservoir; brushy/weedy island edges for crappie; the drowned Welland River channel as the lone deeper cut.

Lake Niapenco is a shallow, weedy, eutrophic reservoir — a largemouth-and-panfish water, not a deep clear bass-or-walleye lake. A 2017 NPCA mark-recapture survey found the sport-fish community dominated by white crappie (~76%, out of pipeline scope) and largemouth bass (~12%), with black crappie (~7%) and smallmouth (~5%) behind, and northern pike and walleye a negligible share. That survey drives the tiering.
