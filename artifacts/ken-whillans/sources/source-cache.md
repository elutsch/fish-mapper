## Ontario Fishing Regulations Summary — FMZ 16 (official, Government of Ontario / MNR)
URL: https://www.ontario.ca/document/ontario-fishing-regulations-summary/fisheries-management-zone-16
Accessed 2026-08-01. Effective date on page: **December 08, 2025**.

- Largemouth and Smallmouth Bass (combined): Season "fourth Saturday in June to November 30"; Limits S-6 and C-2; no size restriction.
- Northern Pike: Season "January 1 to March 31 and second Saturday in May to December 31"; Limits S-6 and C-2; no size restriction.
- Yellow Perch: Season "open all year"; Limits S-50 and C-25; no size restriction.
- FMZ 16 is part of the Southern Bait Management Zone: "Live or dead baitfish or leeches may not be transported into or out of a BMZ."
- FMZ 16 is the most southerly inland fisheries management zone; the Credit River in the Town of Caledon (Peel) is covered under FMZ 16 (Caledon-area Credit River exceptions listed in the FMZ 16 summary), confirming Caledon = FMZ 16.

## Credit Valley Conservation — Fishing Regulations (official)
URL: https://cvc.ca/fishing-regulations/
Accessed 2026-08-01.

- Regulations apply to Fisheries Management Zone 16 (Credit River) and Zone 20 (Lake Ontario); anglers must refer to provincial rules at ontario.ca/fishing.
- Bait: "No outside baitfish are permitted at CVC parks except baitfish purchased at the Island Lake shop." Ken Whillans: worms permitted in ponds only, "not allowed when fishing the streams." Prohibited everywhere: "baitfish, frogs, leeches or crayfish."
- Ken Whillans — Bass: "Catch and release."
- Ken Whillans — Trout: "Catch and release exception for ages under 17 and over 64: 2 with a sport licence, 1 with a conservation licence." (trout season fourth Saturday in April to September 30)
- Fishing during hours of operation.

## Visit Caledon — Fishing in Caledon (municipal tourism)
URL: https://www.visitcaledon.ca/pages/fishing
Accessed 2026-08-01.
- Ken Whillans has two ponds to catch "bass, perch and recently stocked trout."
- "Bass season opens the fourth Saturday in June." CVC "recommended catch and release policies within these ponds."
- "Use the pier or rent a canoe or kayak for a leisurely paddle on the calm waters."
- Trout keep rule: only children under 18 and seniors over 65 may keep trout in season; all others catch-and-release or contribute to the child/senior possession limit. Trout season fourth Saturday in April to September 30. Bass season fourth Saturday in June to November 30, all bass catch and release. Ice fishing not permitted at Ken Whillans.

## Hiking the GTA — Ken Whillans Resource Management Area (blog — user-contributed)
URL: https://hikingthegta.com/2016/11/17/ken-whillans-resource-management-area/
Accessed 2026-08-01.
- "Kidd Pond, after the original landowner, and the other is named Orchard Pond after the part of the homestead it covers."
- Harvest Trail runs 2.3 km around both ponds; a small stream connects them on the western end.
- Park on the former Kidd property; a conservation area since 1954.
- No documented geological origin (kettle / gravel pit / spring-fed) for the ponds.

## Ontario's Conservation Areas — Ken Whillans R.M.A. (directory)
URL: https://ontarioconservationareas.ca/conservation-areas/ken-whillans-r-m-a/
Accessed 2026-08-01.
- 215-acre park, Caledon; west side of Highway 10 (Hurontario St), ~2 km north of Olde Base Line Road.
- Open May–Oct; paid admission; free parking; rentals available.
- Named after Ken Whillans, 46th mayor of Brampton (1982–1990, drowned 1990).
