## Zone-wide species regulations (verbatim extract)

- **Largemouth and Smallmouth Bass (combined)** — Season: "Fourth Saturday in June to November 30" — Limits: "S-6 and C-2" — Size: none specified
- **Crappie** — Season: "Open all year" — Limits: "S-30 and C-10" — Size: none specified
- **Yellow Perch** — Season: "Open all year" — Limits: "S-50 and C-25" — Size: none specified
- **Sunfish** — Season: "Open all year" — Limits: "S-50 and C-25" — Size: none specified
- **Northern Pike** — Season: "January 1 to March 31 and second Saturday in May to December 31" — Limits: "S-6 and C-2" — Size: none specified
- **Walleye and Sauger (combined)** — Season: "January 1 to March 15 and second Saturday in May to December 31" — Limits: "S-4 and C-2; not more than 1 greater than 46 centimetres"

## Bait management zone
- FMZ 16 is part of the Southern Bait Management Zone (BMZ). "Live or dead baitfish or leeches may not be transported into or out of a BMZ."
