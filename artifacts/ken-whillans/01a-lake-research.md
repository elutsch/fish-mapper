# Lake Research: Ken Whillans Resource Management Area

Ken Whillans is not a lake — it is two small managed ponds (Orchard Pond and Kidd Pond) inside a
215-acre Credit Valley Conservation resource management area in Caledon, off Highway 10 (Hurontario
Street). The primary draw is a **stocked put-and-take rainbow trout** fishery (trout stocked each
spring in Orchard Pond), which is **out of this pipeline's six-gamefish scope** and carries no tier
and no species entry. In scope: the warmwater fish documented in the ponds — largemouth bass (Kidd
Pond), northern pike (Orchard Pond), and perch. This is a small, thin fishery; the record reflects
that honestly.
