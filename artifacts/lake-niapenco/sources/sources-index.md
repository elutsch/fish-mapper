# Cached / Consulted Sources — Lake Niapenco

Full-page caching was not available via the fetch tool for most pages; this index records every URL fetched and used, with what each provided. The Biotactic PDF binary and the NPCA fishing chart PDF were downloaded to the tool-results cache during fetch.

- **Angler's Atlas — Lake Niapenco (Zone 16)** — https://www.anglersatlas.com/place/124339/lake-niapenco — surface area 168 ha (~415 acres); species list (8 documented); most-popular catches (smallmouth, largemouth, common carp); confirms FMZ 16.
- **Biotactic Inc. — Lake Niapenco Sport Fish Population Estimates (2017, for NPCA)** — https://www.biotactic.com/lake-niapenco-sport-fish-population-estimates/ — 2017 mark-recapture study; sport-fish community: white crappie 76%, largemouth 12%, black crappie 7%, smallmouth 5%, northern pike ~0%, walleye ~0%; total sport fish 27,149 (95% CI 13,147–59,465).
- **NPCA — Binbrook Conservation Area (ice-fishing / rules page)** — https://npca.ca/parks-recreation/conservation-areas/binbrook/ice-fishing.htm — catch-and-release encouraged; PFAS/mercury/PCB consumption advisory; non-motorized + electric trolling motors only (no gas motors); no motorized vehicles on ice; Zone 16 governs.
- **NPCA — Binbrook Conservation Area (main)** — https://npca.ca/parks-recreation/conservation-areas/binbrook — day-use fee ($15 vehicle; $9 off-season); paddlecraft rentals; catch-and-release encouraged; consumption advisory.
- **Ontario.ca — FMZ 16 regulations summary** — https://www.ontario.ca/document/ontario-fishing-regulations-summary/fisheries-management-zone-16 — official seasons/limits (effective 2025-12-08); Southern Bait Management Zone; no Lake Niapenco waterbody exception.
- **Ontario.ca — FMZ 16 zone page** — https://www.ontario.ca/page/fisheries-management-zone-16-fmz-16 — FMZ 16 is the most southerly inland zone; includes Hamilton, Toronto, Kitchener-Waterloo, London.
- **Kayak Ontario — Lake Niapenco, Hamilton** — https://kayak-ontario.com/lake-niapenco-hamilton/ — long and narrow with many small islands and offshoots; Pickerel Island (bald eagle nest, off-limits); cottages on shores; choppy in wind; created 1971 by damming the Welland River.
- **Openwaterpedia — Lake Niapenco** — https://www.openwaterpedia.com/wiki/Lake_Niapenco — dam built 1971 for summer low-flow augmentation and seasonal flood control; largest inland lake in the Niagara District; in southern Hamilton (Binbrook).
- **Ontario Family Fishing — Binbrook Conservation Area** — https://www.ontariofamilyfishing.com/events/binbrook-conservation-area-fishing-destination-2/ — species list; wheelchair-accessible fishing pier; boat launch; canoe/kayak rental; yellow perch noted catch-and-release; one of the largest interior lake reservoirs in the Niagara Peninsula.
- **Welland River Eutrophication Study (NPCA / Niagara River RAP)** — http://ourniagarariver.ca/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/Welland-Eutrophication-Study-Final-Report.pdf — Lake Niapenco (Binbrook Reservoir) is a section of the eutrophic Welland River system; high phosphorus loads from Beaver Creek and Big Forks Creek sub-watersheds.
