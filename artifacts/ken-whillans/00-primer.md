# Lake Primer

## Physical character
Two small managed ponds — Orchard Pond and Kidd Pond — inside the Ken Whillans Resource Management Area in Caledon, off Highway 10. A small stream links the ponds, and the Credit River runs through the west side of the property. Pond origin (kettle, former aggregate pit, or dug/impounded) is not documented; the closest schema fit for these constructed conservation-area ponds is `reservoir`, an imperfect label.

## Morphology signal
Small, calm warm-water ponds — paddle-scale, not lake-scale. Shallow, weedy warm-water character consistent with a stocked-pond fishery. No documented depth, clarity, or trophic figures. This is put-and-take pond water, not a deep clear basin.

## Watershed & access
Part of the upper Credit River watershed, managed by Credit Valley Conservation as a fee-access resource management area. Carry-in paddlecraft access on Orchard Pond; small fishing piers on both ponds; canoe/kayak rentals; paid admission.

## Managing authorities
- Credit Valley Conservation (CVC)
- Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources (FMZ 16 regulations)

## Fishery reputation signal
Best known as a family, put-and-take rainbow-trout pond fishery (trout stocked each spring in Orchard Pond) — that headline species is out of this pipeline's six-gamefish scope. In-scope warmwater fish present: largemouth bass (Kidd Pond), northern pike (Orchard Pond), and perch. All bass fishing on the property is catch-and-release.
