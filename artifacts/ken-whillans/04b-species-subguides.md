# Species Sub-Guides: Ken Whillans Resource Management Area

## Largemouth Bass
**Parent slug:** largemouth-bass
**Tier:** Strong

### Lede
Kidd Pond is the largemouth's pond — the smaller, quieter of the two, set aside for bass and rigged with a little fishing pier to work it from. It's warm, calm, shallow water, and the fish that suits it best is a largemouth. One thing sets the tone before you cast: every bass at Ken Whillans is catch-and-release, so this is a fish-for-the-fight pond, not a fill-the-cooler one.

### How it fishes
This is small-pond largemouth fishing, and the season sets the rhythm. The bass season opens the fourth Saturday in June and runs to November 30, so summer through fall is your window on Kidd Pond. Largemouth hold in the shallow, weedy cover a warm pond grows, sliding into shade and edges to ambush panfish and anything else that fits a wide mouth. Fish the pier and the reachable shoreline; there's no boating on Kidd Pond, so it's a bank game. And because CVC runs bass as catch-and-release across the property, plan every fish to go back — barbless and a quick release keep the pond's bass healthy for the next angler.

### Structure
- **Kidd Pond**: The RMA's designated largemouth pond — small, warm and weedy, with a fishing pier for shore access. It's the pond to fish for bass; boating is not offered here, so cover the pier and the bank.

### Rules for this species
- Season: fourth Saturday in June to November 30 — Limit: S-6 / C-2 — Size: no size limit (FMZ 16 provincial regulation) — https://www.ontario.ca/document/ontario-fishing-regulations-summary/fisheries-management-zone-16 (effective 2025-12-08)
- CVC property rule (not provincial law): all bass fishing at Ken Whillans is catch-and-release. Worms are permitted in the ponds only (not in the streams); no outside baitfish. — https://cvc.ca/fishing-regulations/ (effective 2025-12-08)

## Northern Pike
**Parent slug:** northern-pike
**Tier:** Strong

### Lede
Orchard Pond is the pike pond — and the busy one. It's where Credit Valley drops rainbow trout each spring for the put-and-take season, and it's where the resident northerns live all year. It's also the only water here you can paddle: carry a canoe or kayak down from the lot, or fish the pier. That mix of stocked trout and standing pike gives early season its edge.

### How it fishes
Pike at Ken Whillans are an Orchard Pond fish, and the pond's dual role shapes the year. When the spring trout go in, resident pike are sharing the same skinny water — an early-season overlap worth knowing. Through spring, summer and fall, work the weedy edges and shoreline where a pike can hang and ambush; the shallow, warm pond gives them cover and forage in the same places. The real advantage over the bass pond is access: Orchard Pond takes carry-in canoes and kayaks, so you can leave the pier and cover the shoreline a cast at a time. Don't plan a winter trip, though — ice fishing is not permitted at Ken Whillans, so the pike season effectively ends when the park closes for the year.

### Structure
- **Orchard Pond**: The RMA's pike water, and the only pond open to boats — carry a canoe or kayak in from the parking lot, or fish the small pier. It doubles as the spring-stocked rainbow trout pond, so early-season anglers share the water.

### Rules for this species
- Season: January 1 to March 31 and second Saturday in May to December 31 — Limit: S-6 / C-2 — Size: no size limit (FMZ 16 provincial regulation) — https://www.ontario.ca/document/ontario-fishing-regulations-summary/fisheries-management-zone-16 (effective 2025-12-08)
- CVC property rule (not provincial law): ice fishing is not permitted at Ken Whillans, so there is no on-site winter pike season despite the January–March provincial window. Fishing only during hours of operation. — https://cvc.ca/fishing-regulations/ (effective 2025-12-08)
