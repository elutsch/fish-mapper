# Ken Whillans Resource Management Area

## Overview
Ken Whillans is a two-pond fishing park off Highway 10 in Caledon, run by Credit Valley Conservation on the old Kidd property. The headline here is the family stuff: Credit Valley stocks Orchard Pond with rainbow trout every spring for a put-and-take season, and that's what most people come for. But two warmwater fish live here year to year. Kidd Pond is the largemouth pond — a small pier, calm water, and bass you have to put back, because all bass fishing on the property is catch-and-release. Orchard Pond holds northern pike alongside the trout, and it's the only pond you can paddle: carry a canoe or kayak down from the lot, or work it off the pier. A stream links the two ponds at the west end, where the Credit River clips the property. It's small, it's shallow, and there's no ice season — CVC doesn't allow ice fishing here.

## Notable Facts
- Ken Whillans Resource Management Area is a 215-acre Credit Valley Conservation park in Caledon, on the former Kidd property, a conservation area since 1954; it is named for Ken Whillans, the 46th mayor of Brampton. — https://ontarioconservationareas.ca/conservation-areas/ken-whillans-r-m-a/
- The property holds two ponds — Kidd Pond, named for the original landowner, and Orchard Pond, named for the homestead orchard — connected by a small stream at the west end, with the Credit River running through the west side. — https://hikingthegta.com/2016/11/17/ken-whillans-resource-management-area/
- The headline fishery is a spring-stocked, put-and-take rainbow trout program in Orchard Pond; the in-scope warmwater fish are largemouth bass in Kidd Pond and northern pike in Orchard Pond. — https://ourroutes.ca/fishing-adventures-in-our-backyard/
- All bass fishing on the property is catch-and-release under CVC property rules, and ice fishing is not permitted. — https://cvc.ca/fishing-regulations/
- Both ponds have small fishing piers; Orchard Pond also allows carry-in canoe/kayak fishing, and the park runs the TackleShare rod-loan program. — https://cvc.ca/enjoy-the-outdoors/conservation-areas/ken-whillans-resource-management-area/

## Largemouth Bass
**Tier:** Strong
**Best Season:** Summer through fall, from the fourth-Saturday-of-June opener
**Structure:** Kidd Pond — the designated largemouth pond, with a small fishing pier

Kidd Pond is the bass pond, full stop. It's small, warm and calm — the kind of water largemouth settle into — and it's set aside for them, pier and all. From the late-June opener through fall they hold in the shallow, weedy cover a pond like this grows, chasing panfish and whatever else fits in a bucket mouth. One rule you can't skip: every bass here goes back. CVC runs the whole property as catch-and-release for bass, so bring a camera, not a stringer, and fish it for the fight.

## Northern Pike
**Tier:** Strong
**Best Season:** Spring through fall; no winter season (ice fishing not permitted)
**Structure:** Orchard Pond — pike water with a small pier, and the only pond you can paddle

Orchard Pond does double duty. It's where Credit Valley drops the spring rainbow trout, and it's where the northerns live the rest of the year — which makes early season interesting, with fresh stockers and resident pike sharing the same skinny water. It's also the only pond open to boats, so you can carry a canoe or kayak down from the lot and cover the shoreline instead of casting from the pier. Work the weedy edges where a pike can sit and ambush. No ice option, though: the season closes when the park does for winter.

Also swimming down there: yellow perch, cruising the same shallow pond water.
